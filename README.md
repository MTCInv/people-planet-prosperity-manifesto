# People, Planet, Prosperity — Manifesto Poster

A first-person manifesto poster on **People, Planet and Prosperity**, aimed at animals and the living planet. Written as a personal stance (I Believe / I Reject / I Demand / I Imagine), not as an educational fact sheet.

## Brief

Course design brief asked for a manifesto: what you stand for, who it is for, and what you want that audience to do. Feedback on an earlier draft called it too educational; this version rewrites the copy in manifesto voice and redesigns the layout.

Audience: students, shoppers and future makers in the same generation — people who decide what gets bought, designed and protected.

Personal line used on the poster:

> I grew up watching the birds, the bees and the animals around me slowly disappear. I decided I would not stay silent.
